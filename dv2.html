Sources & Methodology

Sources: Queensland Art Gallery | Gallery of Modern Art, Collection Artworks and Artists and Organisations (December 2024); City of Melbourne, Public Artworks, Fountains and Monuments; and City of Melbourne Municipal Boundary.

Only exact four-digit creation years are used in creation-date visualisations. Records with missing or uncertain years are omitted from these charts. Missing and unknown creators are excluded from creator rankings.

The proportional symbol and geographic bin maps aggregate public artwork locations into spatial cells. Counts in QAGOMA and Melbourne represent different collections and should not be compared as equivalent national totals.
